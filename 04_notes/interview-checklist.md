## Before coding

- Restate the input, output and legal cases.
- Confirm duplicates, mutation, numeric bounds and impossible results.
- Give a correct baseline and its cost.
- Explain the model and the invariant or recurrence of your improvement.

## While coding

- Use meaningful names and one interval convention.
- Track side effects and shared references.
- Include the actual cost of collection operations.

## Before finishing

- Trace empty/singleton and ordinary cases.
- Test duplicates, extremes and no-solution cases when legal.
- Check time, peak auxiliary memory and reconstruction.
- Explain what changes when one assumption changes.

## After practice

Record the specific mistake, any hint used, a minimal counterexample, and a delayed retry. A personal 45-minute session can use 5 minutes for clarification, 10 for design, 20 for coding and 10 for testing; this is a practice choice, not a Google interview-format claim.
